New and Used Books, Chosen by People Who Actually Read Them

Juniper Lane Bookshop is an independent bookstore on the corner of Juniper and 5th, stocking new releases alongside a carefully curated used section. Every book on our shelves has been read and picked by someone on staff, not ordered off a bestseller list sight unseen.

Stop in Tuesday through Sunday, 10am to 7pm, or browse what is new below. Our monthly book club meets the first Thursday of every month, open to anyone, no sign-up required.
